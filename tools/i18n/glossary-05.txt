Weight	A trainable coefficient in a model transformation. Weights are usually organized into tensors, and optimization adjusts them to reduce the training objective.
Weight Decay	An update rule that reduces selected parameter magnitudes over training, often by multiplying weights by a shrinkage factor separate from the gradient update.	[why] It can improve generalization, but the useful coefficient and excluded parameter groups depend on model, optimizer, schedule, and data.
